HUNT THE WITCH - BAN DEMO MO KHOA

Cach chay
1. Giai nen toan bo thu muc.
2. Mo "Hunt the Witch Demo.exe".
3. Nhan Play, ra Home va den ban che tao.
4. Nhan Space de mo Unlock Menu.
5. Tu mo khoa skill, skin va gay de trinh bay.

Nguyen lieu
- Kho co it nhat 1.000 moi loai nguyen lieu dung trong cac cong thuc mo khoa.
- Khong tu mo khoa san; van giu nut Unlock va viec tieu hao nguyen lieu.
- Moi lan mo lai ung dung, nguyen lieu demo duoc bo sung ve muc demo.
- Khong thay doi mau, damage, loot hay dieu kien danh boss.

Luu game
- Ban demo dung profile-demo-v1.json, tach voi profile-v1.json cua ban thuong.
- Mo khoa trong demo duoc giu lai khi mo lai demo.
- Neu can trinh bay lai viec Unlock tu dau: dong demo va sao luu/doi ten
  profile-demo-v1.json cung profile-demo-v1.json.bak trong thu muc save.
  Khong can xoa file save cua ban choi thuong.

Dieu khien: WASD / phim mui ten, chuot trai ban, Q/E dung skill,
Space tuong tac, Esc tam dung.

Khi gui cho nguoi khac, gui toan bo thu muc, khong chi gui file exe.
